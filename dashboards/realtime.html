Updated every 5 seconds while this page is open (the IoTaWatt averages over 5 s; the stored history stays at one-minute values). House = PV − grid; downstairs = mains A + B + zone C + washer (the third phase has no mains CT); upstairs = house − downstairs, so anything downstairs on the third phase without its own CT shows up as upstairs. Per phase, the inverter's power is split by its current on each phase. MBMD and the IoTaWatt are a few seconds apart: compare steady loads, not the moment something switches.
