Learning: forecasts vs measurements

Forecast errors

Solid lines are measurements, dashed lines forecasts. For each hour the page shows the newest forecast that had been made the chosen time ahead (and, for hours still to come, before now). Errors compare each forecast with the first measurement listed for that chart, over the hours both have. Forecasts are kept from 2025-10 (weather) and from when dorfsteig-forecast started (PV; earlier days are backfilled 24 h ahead).
