Whole-house figures come from the grid meter and inverter (MBMD), the downstairs panel from IoTaWatt (mains A + B, plus zone C and the washer, which are on the third phase, and the second leg of the two-phase water heater, oven and stovetop, which draw twice their channel); upstairs is the difference. The upstairs water heater isn't measured: it's estimated from 2950 W switching steps, so treat daily values as approximate. High tariff: Mon–Fri 07–20, Sat 07–13, except public holidays. Costs only for years with prices (2026 onwards). Hours without data are missing from the totals.
