Heater power

A test pauses PV switching, measures grid power, toggles one heater, measures again and restores it (about 3 minutes). Best done when the house is quiet and nothing else is cycling. Every normal switch also gives a passive estimate; once there are three, they're used instead of the last test. Water heaters have their own thermostat, so a test only measures something while the tank is heating; they're not part of "test all". A test that sees no load is listed below but doesn't change the estimate.
