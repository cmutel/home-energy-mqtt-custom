Water heaters

PV mode: water heaters get PV surplus first (on above {{ (heater_w + water.on_margin_w) | int }} W export, off above {{ water.off_import_w | int }} W import, at least {{ (water.min_on_s / 60) | int }} min on/off). A tank not seen full since the "full since" time heats until full at the backup time, and any tank not seen full for the safety time heats until full. Without grid data the tanks are on. Always-on rules are set aside in PV mode; boosts still work. Shadow mode only logs what PV mode would do (History, dry run).

PV surplus switching (room heaters)

Fit: each zone below has a heat budget for the day, from the day's mean outdoor temperature. Once the water heaters are served, the heater of the zone furthest behind its budget that fits the export minus {{ solar.margin_w | int }} W goes on, one per minute; a zone stops at {{ (solar.max_share * 100) | round | int }}% of its budget. If nothing more fits, a heater on for {{ (solar.rotate_after_s / 60) | int }} min makes way for a zone {{ (solar.rotate_gap * 100) | round | int }} points further behind, so the heat goes round the zones. A heater that draws nothing when switched on (switched off by hand, or core full) is skipped for {{ (solar.no_load_skip_s / 60) | int }} min. Above {{ solar.fit_off_import_w | int }} W import the zone furthest ahead goes off after at least {{ (solar.min_on_s / 60) | int }} min, above {{ solar.fast_shed_w | int }} W at once. Legacy: above the export threshold the next heater in the zone list goes on, below the import threshold the last one goes off. Shadow: legacy, logging what fit would do (History, dry run). Grid power is positive when exporting.

Zones

Budget per day = kWh per °C × (heats below − the day's mean outdoor temperature), nothing on warmer days. Give rooms that get heat from below (the upper floors) a lower "heats below". The day runs from {{ night_params.charge_from.strftime("%H:%M") }} to {{ night_params.charge_from.strftime("%H:%M") }}, so a night charge counts towards the next day; the mean is the station so far plus the forecast, and is updated during the day. "Got" is on-time × measured power for everything that switched the heater on (PV, night plan, boosts){% if solar.mode == "shadow" %}; in shadow mode, what the shadow would have switched{% endif %}.

Night plan (room heaters from the grid)

For colder days, when PV alone isn't enough; off heats from PV only, and the house gets colder on grey days. Every evening at the start of the charging window the plan takes tomorrow's zone budgets (from tomorrow's forecast mean temperature), counts {{ night_params.pv_credit }} × tomorrow's forecast PV surplus (PV beyond the usual house load) against them, shared in proportion to the budgets, and charges the rest of each zone ticked for night charging, except heaters the wattage test found switched off. If that's less than {{ night_params.min_kwh }} kWh in all, nothing is charged. All start at the start of the window, each for as long as its zone needs. Shadow: make and show the plan, log what it would switch (History, dry run), switch nothing.
